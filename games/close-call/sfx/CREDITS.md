# Close Call sound effects

Every sound here is a real recording from [Freesound](https://freesound.org), released under
**Creative Commons 0** (public domain). They were trimmed, looped and level-matched for the game;
the engine loops are slices of dyno runs and an acceleration recording with the pitch held steady.
Credit isn't required under CC0, but thanks to the recordists:
