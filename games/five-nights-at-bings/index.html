How to play

You are Sean, the night guard. Survive from 12 AM to 6 AM without running out of power.

Doors: A / D (or the red buttons). Lights: Q / E. Check the light, shut the door if someone is there, open it when they leave.

Cameras: Space or the bar at the bottom. Number keys 1 to 9 switch cameras.

Music box: on the Prize Corner camera hold WIND (or F). If it stops, Benton wakes up and you have 15 seconds left.

Sal: on the Bar camera hold POUR (or F) to keep his bottle full. If it runs dry he runs at you: shut the door on his side.

Vent: W (or the blue button) seals the vent over the desk when Mr. Tokens crawls close.

New: hold the CRANK on the desk (or G) to make power by hand. It is loud: everyone moves faster while you crank. Once a night it can restart a dead generator. On the cameras, PLAY SOUND (L) lures a robot back toward that room, but it bothers Benton.

Every sound has a caption, so you can play with the sound off. M = sound, C = captions.
