      HOW TO PLAY

      Everyone floats in a ring. A category comes up and one player holds the nova, a star about to blow.

      On your turn, type an answer nobody has used yet. Got one? The nova jumps to the next player.

      The fuse is hidden and it does not reset when the nova is passed. Holding it when it blows costs a life. Last one in orbit wins.

      Spelling is forgiving: capitals, spaces and one small typo are fine.
